# blendCalc User Guide

blendCalc helps you organize food, understand available nutrition information, monitor
recall notices, and surface allergen or dietary concerns based on preferences you
choose. You can also compare food combinations with your nutrition goals. It does not
replace the current package label, an official recall notice, or professional medical
advice.

## Ingredients

Use **Ingredients** to search foods and products, scan a barcode, or enter an item from
its label. Save items to your **Fridge** when you have them or to your **Shopping List**
when you plan to get them. Moving an item between lists does not duplicate it.

Open a food's nutrition details to review every available nutrient, ingredients,
allergen statements, servings, barcode, brand, source attribution, recall information,
and revision details. Missing information is shown as unknown rather than guessed.

## Mix

Use **Mix** to combine saved ingredients and adjust their amounts. The nutrient shape,
goals, warnings, and contribution details update from the selected foods and serving
amounts. A goal is used only when you or a reviewed preset supplies a real target.

Suggestions are guidance, not medical advice. Review every adjustment before applying
it, especially when product data is incomplete.

## Saved Recipes

Save a Mix when you want to use it again. **Saved Recipes** lets you search, sort, open,
load, share, or remove those combinations. Loading a recipe does not overwrite its
saved copy unless you explicitly save the updated version.

## Profile

Use **Profile** to update your details, appearance, measurement preferences, tutorial
access, and food preferences. Allergen and dietary settings influence warning edges,
search context, nutrition details, and Mix warnings when the available evidence can be
evaluated.

## Warnings And Data Confidence

Warnings describe conflicts or safety information found in the data available to
blendCalc. They are not a guarantee that an unflagged food is safe.

- **Amber** asks you to review a possible preference, allergen, or data concern.
- **Red** identifies a serious warning such as an active recall or high-severity issue.
- **Incomplete** means a source did not provide enough evidence for a confident check.
- **Source details** explain where a field came from and when it was last reviewed.

Always check the current package label. Product formulas, package sizes, and recall
coverage can change.

## Privacy And Sharing

Private foods and personal image placement stay connected to your account. A community
submission becomes shared only through the catalog's evidence and publication rules.
Private user details, moderation evidence, and private image paths are not exposed by
the public data contract.
